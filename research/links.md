# Linked pages research — Crumb and Dough

## Confirmed links belonging to the business
- **TikTok: `https://www.tiktok.com/@crumbandough`** — sole link in the Instagram bio, titled “TIK TOK 🍪”. TikTok profile page was blocked by a WAF challenge on direct fetch. A third-party index lists 28 posts, 50 followers, 566 likes (low confidence, not treated as fact).
- **No other link-in-bio page found.** No Linktree, Beacons, Stan Store, Square site, Google Form or order form confirmed.

## Ordering mechanism (confirmed from Instagram captions)
1. Customer DMs @crumbandough.
2. Chooses a box: 6 cookies for $35 AUD every day, or 4 cookies for $24 AUD on Mondays & Tuesdays.
3. Chooses flavours: mix & match, or all six the same.
4. Chooses pickup or delivery (Melbourne).
5. Limited drops require a 50% deposit and have fixed delivery dates.
No automated checkout, payment link or booking system found.
